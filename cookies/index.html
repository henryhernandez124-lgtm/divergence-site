Cookie Policy

Last updated June 07, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how HDZ Capital Group LLC ("Company," "we," "us," and "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website at https://divergenceapp.com ("Website"). It explains what these technologies are, whether we use them, and your rights to control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies set by the website owner are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies," and can enable third-party features such as advertising or analytics.

Does this Website use cookies?

The Divergence website sets no cookies of its own. We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or any other tracking cookies, and we do not run a cookie consent banner because the site sets no cookies that require consent.

The only third parties the Website contacts are:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), which serves the site's typeface. Google Fonts does not set cookies, though it receives your IP address as part of delivering the font files.
  • Google Forms (docs.google.com), which is contacted only when you choose to submit the beta waitlist form. At that point, Google may set its own cookies in connection with processing your submission. This does not happen unless you submit the form.

We do not serve targeted advertising, and we do not use Flash cookies or Local Shared Objects.

What about web beacons?

We do not use web beacons, tracking pixels, or clear gifs on the Website or in our emails.

How can I control cookies?

Because the Website sets no cookies of its own, there is nothing to opt out of here. You can still control or delete cookies — including any cookies Google may set when you submit the waitlist form — through your web browser settings. Most browsers let you refuse or remove cookies through their help or settings menu (for example, Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, and Opera). If you choose to block cookies, some features of websites you visit may not function properly.

How often will we update this Cookie Policy?

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect, for example, changes to the technologies we use or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The date at the top of this Cookie Policy indicates when it was last updated.
